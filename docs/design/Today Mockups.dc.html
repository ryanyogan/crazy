Today — round one

Assumptions. Sample user is Mara Okafor, platform engineering lead at Halyard. Four connected tools (Google, Slack, Linear, Notion). Three work projects and two personal ones. It is Wednesday 08:41.

System. Industry wireframe grammar: square, hairline-framed blueprint objects with registration marks; Barlow Condensed headings; one steel accent. The single solid accent object on every screen is the one thing to do next. Source chips (SL, LN, NO, GM, CAL) are tiny condensed tags. Meetings are tinted fills, focus blocks are framed, free hours are dashed.

Two home takes. 1a leads with the brief, then the hour timeline with todos slotted in. 1b leads with the priority stack and groups todos by energy, nested under projects; the brief becomes a side column. Remaining screens (1c–1g) are single takes. Technical notes for Claude Code are in CLAUDE-CODE-NOTES.md.
